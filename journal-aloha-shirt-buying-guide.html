How to Choose an Aloha Shirt

Fabric, fit and prints: what to look for so your shirt looks good on the beach and at the dinner table.

By the Mahalo Market team · 5 min read

The aloha shirt is one of the most recognizable pieces of island style. Bright prints, a relaxed cut and a short sleeve make it easy to wear on warm days. Finding one you will keep wearing comes down to three things: the fabric, the fit and the print.

A short history of the word

The aloha shirt grew out of Honolulu's warm, relaxed culture and became popular in the 1930s. Many people wore it as an everyday shirt, and it soon became a symbol of the islands. Today it is worn for beach days, weddings, luaus and office Fridays alike.

Choose a fabric that breathes

  • Cotton: soft, breathable and easy to care for. A good all-round choice.
  • Rayon or viscose: smooth and drapes well. Feels cool but can wrinkle more easily.
  • Blends with stretch: comfortable for active days and keep their shape. Look for quick-dry options if you swim or travel often.

Check the care label before you buy. Shirts that can be machine washed are easier to keep fresh through a summer.

Find a fit that feels relaxed

Traditional aloha shirts are cut loose through the body, so they hang comfortably over shorts or trousers. Choose the size that fits your shoulders first. A shirt that is too tight across the chest will look uncomfortable, and one that is too big will look sloppy. If you are between sizes, the larger size usually works better for an aloha cut.

Fit checkLift your arms. The shirt should move with you without pulling at the buttons. That is the real test of a good aloha shirt.

Pick a print that suits you

Prints range from large hibiscus and palm patterns to small geometric designs and vintage-style scenes. A bold print works well for a party or a vacation. A smaller or simpler print is easier to wear with everyday clothes. If you are new to aloha wear, start with a medium-scale print in a color you already wear.

Match the shirt to the day

For a beach day, choose a light cotton or quick-dry fabric. For a luau or evening event, a rayon shirt with a drapey feel looks elegant. For a casual weekend, a cotton shirt with a classic print is the easiest choice.

Pair it with the rest of the island look

A good aloha shirt is the start of a relaxed outfit. Add simple shorts or light trousers, comfortable shoes and a koa wood bracelet for a finishing touch. If you are shopping for someone else, our Hawaiian gift ideas guide has more suggestions for the people in your life.

And for the perfect morning before a day in the sun, pair the look with a cup of 100% Kona coffee. It is a small ritual that many island lovers enjoy.
